### 1. `docs/design/index.md:53-64` — four unrelated designs now sit under "Attention optimization"

```
#### Attention optimization
... Skip-Softmax is the backend-related optimization with a standalone feature-design contract:

- [Skip-Softmax](feature/skip_softmax.md)

- [Cache-DiT](feature/cache_dit.md)
- [TeaCache](feature/teacache.md)
- [Diffusion Continuous Batching](feature/diffusion_continuous_batching.md)
- [Distributed Layerwise Offload](feature/distributed_layerwise_offload.md)
```

Two `-` lists separated by a single blank line with no intervening block merge into one loose list in Python-Markdown — and even if they didn't, all four items still fall under the `#### Attention optimization` heading, immediately after a sentence ending in a colon that introduces Skip-Softmax as *the* backend-related contract. Cache-DiT, TeaCache, continuous batching, and distributed offload are none of those things.

This was already broken pre-PR (they dangled under `#### Quantization`), but this PR moves the heading over them and is specifically about taxonomy correctness, so it's the right place to fix. It also now disagrees with `.nav.yml:127-133`, where those four are siblings of the `Attention Optimization` group, not children.

**Change:** move the four bullets above `#### Parallelism` (directly under `### Diffusion acceleration`), or give them their own subheading such as `#### Caching and scheduling`.

### 2. `docs/.nav.yml:52-62` — GGUF silently dropped from navigation

`docs/user_guide/quantization/gguf.md` still exists and is still presented as a first-class method in pages that *are* in nav:

- `docs/user_guide/quantization/overview.md:49, 66, 82` — three tables linking `[GGUF](gguf.md)`
- `docs/user_guide/diffusion_features.md:95` — `| **[GGUF](quantization/gguf.md)** | quantization="gguf" | ... |`

So the Quantization nav section now lists every supported method except this one, and the page is reachable only by clicking through a table. `--strict` won't catch it: `validation.nav.omitted_files` defaults to INFO. Worth noting that BitsAndBytes was removed and then restored within this same PR (`e63ae881` → `b112c7fd`); GGUF looks like the same slip, un-restored. It's also the one change in the diff not mentioned in the PR description.

**Change:** restore `- GGUF: user_guide/quantization/gguf.md` to the Quantization group. If the intent really was to retire it (it's migrated to the OOT `vllm-gguf-plugin`), that's a separate, described change that also has to update the three `overview.md` tables and `diffusion_features.md:95`.
