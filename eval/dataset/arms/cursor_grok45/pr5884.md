Any future pipeline that lists nested offload-only modules in `_dit_modules` without a custom enabler would incorrectly enable Cache-DiT on those modules. Worth documenting when adding the next Cosmos3-like model; not a defect in Bagel’s path (`language_model.model` → `Qwen2MoTModel` with `_cache_dit_adapter_config`).
